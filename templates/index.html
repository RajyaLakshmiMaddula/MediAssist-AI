NLP stages from raw sentence to ranked match

How your sentence becomes a result

Every stage is visible in your result page, so you can see exactly why the system landed where it did rather than trusting a number.

Stage 1

Tokenise

The sentence is cleaned and split into individual words.

Stage 2

Strip filler

Common words go, but negations like "no" and "without" are kept.

Stage 3

Lemmatise and tag

"coughing" becomes "cough", then each word gets a part of speech.

Stage 4

Map to symptoms

"loose motions" and "watery stools" both resolve to diarrhea.

Stage 5

Grade severity

Intensity words and duration set how urgent the picture looks.

Stage 6

Score conditions

Rare, telling symptoms count for more than common ones.

Stage 7

Build the plan

Medicines, food, precautions and the department to visit.

It knows what you ruled out

Write "fever and body ache but no cough" and cough is recorded as denied, not detected. Conditions that depend on it get pushed down the ranking instead of up.

Emergencies skip the ranking

Phrases pointing to a stroke, heart attack or breathing crisis stop the analysis and show one instruction: get help now. A confidence percentage is the wrong answer to that sentence.

Every result is explained

You see which symptoms matched, which are missing, what else was considered and how close the runner-up was. Low confidence is reported as low confidence.

Reports you can hand over

Download any consultation as a PDF and take it to your appointment. It is a structured summary of what you reported, not a prescription.

This is not a diagnosis. MediAssist AI is an educational project. It cannot examine you, order a blood test, or know your medical history and current medication. Symptom overlap between serious and trivial conditions is enormous, and software pattern-matching cannot tell them apart. Use this to organise your thoughts before seeing a doctor. If you are worried, in pain, or getting worse, contact a qualified doctor or your local emergency number.
